Why these talents?
  • Implosion, not Power Siphon: your only on-demand AoE, up to 6 imps every 15 s.
  • Vilefiend comes out with every F for free, and Reign of Tyranny makes Tyrant and the dogs last longer.
  • Raid vs cleave: only two swaps. Practiced Rituals ↔ Dreadlash, Mark of Shatug (single target) ↔ F'harg (AoE).
  • M+ class talents: Shadowfury (AoE stun) and Blight of Tongues (slower casts, easier kicks).
  • Diabolist: Wowhead's pick everywhere, for its passive AoE and better defensives.

Where your damage comes from

Your demons do the damage. You summon them, then make them stronger.

  • E makes Wild Imps: your biggest damage source.
  • F makes Dreadstalkers + Vilefiend.
  • 2 Tyrant buffs every demon that's out. More demons = bigger Tyrant.
  • Mastery raises demon damage.
Diabolist bonus demons (they come by themselves)

Spending shards fills Diabolic Ritual. When it ends, your next E summons a big demon, always in this order: Overlord (+5% damage taken) → Mother of Chaos (Q becomes Infernal Bolt, 3 shards) → Pit Lord (E becomes Ruination, +3 imps). Each one also drops Demonic Oculi that explode for AoE and give you Intellect. You press nothing new: watch Q and E change.

Getting stronger

Now

  1. Higher item level wins. Equip it even with worse stats.
  2. Stats when ilvl ties: Haste = Crit > Mastery > Vers.
  3. Before keys and raids: Flask of the Shattered Sun, Thalassian Phoenix Oil, Royal Roast.
  4. Potions in bags: Light's Potential (⇧2), Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion (⇧C).

Later

  1. Sim yourself on Raidbots once you have a few upgrades.
  2. Craft Arcanoweave Lining as your embellishment.
  3. Catalyst tier pieces. The 4-piece gives you free Implosions.
  4. Nebulous Voidcore bonus rolls drop Myth-track items at +10 keys or Heroic raid bosses.
  5. Chase the trinkets Gebbo's Bottomless Bag and Vile Vial of Volatile Venom.
